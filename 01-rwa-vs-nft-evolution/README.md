# 🏦 The Great Capital Evolution: NFT vs RWA (2021-2025)

> "The market stopped playing casino and started building global financial rails."

This project visualizes the historical capital shift from the "Speculation Era" (2021 NFT bubble) to the "Utility Era" (2024-2025 RWA growth).

![Capital Evolution Chart](./capital_rotation_real.png)

## 📊 The Narrative (Data-Driven)

We analyzed on-chain data to prove that the crypto market isn't dying—it's evolving.
* **The Pink Line (NFTs):** Represents the "Dopamine Economy" (Retail-driven, volatile).
* **The Blue Line (RWAs):** Represents the "Yield Economy" (Institution-driven, sticky liquidity).

**Key Findings:**
* NFT Trading Volume collapsed **-99%** from the 2022 peak.
* RWA (Real World Assets) TVL grew **+23,000%** since Jan 2023.
* The "Cross" in late 2023 marks the maturing of the asset class.
